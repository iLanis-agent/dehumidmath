The boundary

Dehumid Math keeps labeled-norm arithmetic only. Pint ratings, the 2012-to-2019 standard shift and room sizing figures are published test-lab norms, not promises about your basement - real pull depends on temperature, humidity, airflow and the unit itself. Follow the manufacturer's sizing guidance and manual over anything this page says, and for mold or water coming in, talk to a professional.

In 2019 the test changed. A dehumidifier's pint count is measured in a lab room, and the 2019 lab room is cooler than the 2012 one - so today's 50-pint box is yesterday's 70-pint box, and your basement is cooler than either lab. Dehumid Math does the honest arithmetic between the labels, the room and the bucket.

Three panels. The label truth translates between the 2012 and 2019 pint scales. The room truth weighs the room's size and dampness against the unit's label. The bucket truth counts the hours between emptyings.
